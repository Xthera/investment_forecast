The bank rate is an editable placeholder: Singapore has no single average, and 12-month fixed deposits and Singapore Savings Bonds recently sit around 1% to 2% a year, while ordinary savings accounts can pay as little as 0.05%. Illustration only, not financial advice. Both the expected return and the bank rate are treated as annual rates that compound, so each year's growth builds on the previous balance, with the growth applied monthly to match your contribution timing; real markets fluctuate and can lose money. A single premium is paid once at the start. Otherwise contributions are made at the end of each period for the contribution years only, then the money stays invested until the end of the horizon.
